Early access privacy

Updated 12 September 2026. This notice covers the MaybeSitter early access registration website, not the application's future data practices.

What we collect and why

We collect your first name, email, device preference, optional phone number, registration time and campaign source to manage the waiting list and contact you about early access. The phone number is optional. We do not collect your goals, tasks or commitments through this form.

Storage and access

Details are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the europe-west1 region. Access is restricted to authorized project operators and the registration service. Your registration is not publicly viewable and is not sold.

Aggregate activity

We count page visits, early access clicks, started forms and successful registrations by day and campaign. Browser activity counts respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not add advertising trackers, tracking cookies or visitor identifiers. The hosting providers may process technical request logs to operate and secure the service.

Your choices

We retain your registration while managing early access unless you request removal. You can request access, correction or deletion, or ask a registration question, by emailing anasakkari04@gmail.com. Please use the address you registered with so we can verify your request.
