How it works

  1. Type it the way you'd say it

    In Arabic, Hebrew or English, whichever language you think in.

  2. Confirm it

    A captured commitment is saved only after you confirm it. If something is missing, it asks.

  3. It shows up in your day

    You see it in your day at its time, and you get a reminder before it's due when notifications are enabled.

What's different

  • No overdue pile

    Everything is done, moved, or dropped on purpose. The app is built without an “overdue” state.

  • Designed to interrupt less

    Quiet hours, and a limit on how often it interrupts you. That's a design choice we're still testing with real people.

  • Your words, your confirmation

    You type in your own language, and a commitment is saved only after you confirm it.

You decide

  • AI features run only after you say yes. Say no and the app still works with simple rules.
  • You can see, edit and delete what it keeps.
  • No ad tracking.
  • Usage analytics only if you turn them on in the app. They start off.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Privacy policy · Delete your account and data

Who it's for

People who study and work, and make promises in chats, classes and shifts.

It isn't for managing projects or teams, it isn't an assistant that acts on your behalf, and it isn't a medical tool.

Where we are

We're early. There are no ratings or user numbers yet, and we won't invent any. We're starting with a small test in Israel and learning from the people who actually use it. When we have real numbers, they'll be here.

Questions

Which languages?

The app's interface is in Arabic, Hebrew and English, and you type in whichever language you think in.

How much does it cost?

It's free during early access. We haven't decided on pricing after that.

Where can I get it?

It's coming to the App Store and Google Play in Israel. We're starting with a closed test for a small group.

Does it read my messages or email?

No. It doesn't connect to your email or your messages.

Does it use AI?

Only if you say yes. If you say no, it works with simple rules. The details are in the privacy policy.

Will it flood me with notifications?

A reminder comes before something is due, and notifications have to be enabled. There are quiet hours and a limit on interruptions.

How is it different from a notes app or a task app?

Notes don't come back to you. Many task apps grow an “overdue” list. Here there is no overdue state: things are done, moved, or dropped on purpose.

How do I delete my data?

From inside the app, or by asking us. The steps are on the account deletion page.
