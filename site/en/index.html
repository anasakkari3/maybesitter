MaybeSitter

MaybeSitter helps you remember what you promised to do, and reminds you at the right time.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What it is

You promise things all day — in a chat, in a hallway, on a call. MaybeSitter catches those promises, keeps them in one place, and brings each one back when it is actually useful, not at a random hour.

Nothing is saved without your confirmation, and you can read, edit and delete everything MaybeSitter remembers about you.
