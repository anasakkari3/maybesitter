Delete your account

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

You can delete your MaybeSitter account and everything in it at any time, without asking anyone. This page exists so you can find out how without installing the app.

From inside the app

Settings → Trust centre → Delete my account. It asks you to confirm, then deletes immediately and shows you a receipt with a reference you can keep.

If you signed in with Apple, this also revokes MaybeSitter's access to your Apple ID, so it disappears from Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple on your device.

If you no longer have the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my MaybeSitter account.

We reply from that address only, and we do it within 30 days. We ask you to write from the signed-in address for one reason: it is how we know the request is yours. If you cannot, tell us and we will find another way to check — we will not simply take your word for it, because doing that for you means doing it for anyone who claims to be you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Every commitment, reminder and capture.
  • Everything MaybeSitter remembered about you: your routine answers and every memory fact, including the ones you edited.
  • Your consents, your settings and your feedback history.
  • Any calendar data imported to your account, and the permission to read your calendar, which is revoked.
  • Your device's notification token.

This is a real delete, not a flag. Once it is done we cannot restore it, and neither can you — which is the point.

What is not deleted, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups, up to 30 days. They exist so a failure on our side cannot lose everyone's data. They are overwritten on that cycle and are never read to restore a deleted account.
  • Crash reports, up to 90 days. These are not linked to your identity — no account id is attached — so there is nothing in them to find you by.

We are naming these because a deletion page that says "everything is gone" and quietly keeps backups is the kind of accurate-sounding sentence nobody can check.
