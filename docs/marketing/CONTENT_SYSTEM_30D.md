# The first 30 days of content: a system, not a calendar of posts

Window: the tester-recruitment period, roughly 2026-10-01 → 2026-10-30. The job of this
content is **consented testers, especially people who don't know the founder**. Reach for
its own sake doesn't count.

Nothing here auto-publishes. The owner approves and posts every piece. Every product
statement must pass [`CLAIMS_POLICY.md`](CLAIMS_POLICY.md) before it is drafted, not after.

## Rules that apply to every piece

- **No "overwhelmed by tasks" content.** No stock "stressed person at a laptop", and no
  "too many tasks?" hooks. Every piece is about a specific moment, a specific decision, or
  a specific thing the product does that the claims policy allows.
- **No generative visuals of the app.** Never let an image or video model redraw Arabic
  (or any) UI text. App footage is real screen recordings from a synthetic test account.
  Generative imagery, if used at all, carries no UI and no text.
- **Only features the claims policy allows.** Today that is: typing a commitment, the
  confirm step, it showing up in your day, reminders *when notifications are enabled*,
  done / moved / dropped, the AI consent choice, see/edit/delete. No voice, no dialect
  claims, no calendars, no email, no goals, no widget.
- **Survey numbers** always carry "small survey, 41 people, mostly students".
- **No invented people.** Scenarios are written as scenarios ("say you promised…"), never
  as testimonials. Tester quotes appear only with explicit, per-quote consent, anonymised.
- **Funding** appears only if the accelerator's terms allow it, named accurately, and
  never as proof of traction (X14). Until then, build-in-public posts leave it out.
- Every post links with its own `?v=` arm and `?source=` code (see
  [`TESTER_RECRUITMENT_KIT.md`](TESTER_RECRUITMENT_KIT.md)).
- Hebrew posts wait for native review (#335).

## The five pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real promise moments | ~35% | A specific promise from a chat, a hallway or a shift, and what happened to it | Generic productivity advice |
| Say it your way | ~25% | Real typed sentences in Arabic, Hebrew or English, and what the app did with them, **including misses** | Claims about dialect or voice |
| Build in public | ~20% | Decisions and evidence: what we cut, what we're testing (the A/B lines), what we measure, what went wrong | Funding brags, vanity numbers |
| Trust and control | ~10% | The confirm step, the AI choice, see/edit/delete, what the website doesn't collect | "100% private" or any absolute |
| Research findings | ~10% | One finding per piece from the survey (labelled directional), later from tester interviews (consented) | Findings stretched past their sample |

## The weekly rhythm

Israel's week starts on Sunday.

| Day | Slot | Channel | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Founder post | LinkedIn | English (sometimes Arabic) |
| Tue | Reel, 20–40 s | Instagram | Arabic, captions on |
| Thu | Carousel, 5–7 frames | Instagram | Arabic |
| Most days | 2–3 Stories / WhatsApp Status | Instagram, WhatsApp | Arabic |
| Weekly | Update to people who asked to hear more | Email or opt-in WhatsApp broadcast (never a group) | Their language |

The rhythm is 3 fixed pieces a week plus low-effort stories. If time runs short, drop the
stories first, never the Tuesday Reel.

## The four weeks

Each week has a theme. Each slot has a pillar and the claim IDs it may use (see the
claims policy).

| Week | Theme | Sun (LinkedIn) | Tue (Reel) | Thu (Carousel) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The promise | Build in public: why we stopped selling "AI" and started testing two plain lines (A/B) | Promise moment: a chat promise → typed → confirm → in the day (A2, A8, Q2) | Research: "most misses weren't forgetting" (Q8) |
| 2 | Say it your way | Build in public: what "confirm before save" costs us and why we keep it (A2) | Say it your way: three real sentences, one it got wrong, and what we changed | Promise moment: moved, not failed; done / moved / dropped on purpose (A7, Q1 as design only) |
| 3 | Trust and control | Trust: what this website does not collect, and why (no cookies, no analytics) | Trust: the AI choice at the start; saying no still works (A3) | Trust: see, edit, delete (Q10) |
| 4 | Join the test | Build in public: what testers actually do (14 days, nothing to fill in) | Promise moment: a shift-week story, with the join link | Recap and join: who we're looking for (18+, Android especially) |

Stories through the month: a poll ("What did you promise someone today?"), behind the
scenes of recording, the week's post reshared, and a "join the test" sticker with the
week's arm link.

## Hooks that fit the rules (drafts to adapt, not posts to copy)

- "You said 'I'll send it tomorrow.' Where did that promise go?"
- «قلتله "بحكيلك بكرة". وين راح هالوعد؟»
- "We're testing two sentences. Tell us which one you understand in 5 seconds."
- "Nothing turns red here. That's a design decision, and here's why we made it."
- «ما في إشي بصير أحمر هون. هاد قرار، وهاد ليش.»
- "It asked me something before saving. That's the point."

## The repurposing chain

1. Once a week, record a 60–90 second real screen session on a synthetic account: typed
   capture → confirm → it's in the day → moved or dropped.
2. Cut it into: 1 Arabic-captioned Reel · 1 LinkedIn clip (English caption) · 3 Stories ·
   1 carousel frame · candidate store screenshots (for later ASO work).
3. One founder note becomes: LinkedIn post → Arabic carousel → an FAQ answer on the site
   (the FAQ change goes through the site's normal review and claims check).

## Production workflow

1. **Sunday, 2 hours:** batch the week. Draft, run the claims check on every sentence and
   every frame, and prepare the arm link and source code for each piece.
2. **Owner approval:** read every piece before it goes out.
3. **Post manually.** No scheduler that publishes without a person pressing the button.
4. **Mid-week, 30 minutes:** read the numbers below.

## What we measure, and the kill rule

- **Per piece:** saves plus shares per reach; link taps (platform-reported).
- **Per week:** tester sign-ups by `source` and `v`; consented testers from outside the
  founder's network. That last one is the number that matters.
- **Kill rule:** a pillar averaging under about 1% saves-plus-shares per reach after four
  pieces gets replaced by more of the best-performing pillar.
- Report with denominators, and never turn a week's small numbers into a trend claim in
  public.
