MaybeSitter · round 1 · prototype

Capture, the day, and what replaces “overdue”

One connected prototype in the iPhone frame: voice-first capture through review, clarification, two readings and saved-with-undo; the day timeline and week calendar; and the three flows that replace late/overdue: close-out, rearrange, first move. Arabic-first, English mirrored. Light and dark.

Decisions applied from your answers
  • Tabs: Today · Calendar · Capture · Settings. Activity moves to Settings.
  • Importance kept as soft badges, no filters. “Must” is warm sand, not red.
  • Both the next-step card and the first-move flow live in the day view.
  • Spoken Arabic for actions: تمّت · لسّا · احكيها · أسقطه بوعي.
  • Progress kept, as plain words with a thin bar. Never a percentage.
System (fresh identity)
Neutral greys, one teal accent for actions and “done”, one warm sand for “must”. Noto Naskh Arabic for Arabic, Outfit for Latin. Radii 20 / 28 / 36 (pillowy). Busy blocks are hatched grey with no titles. Nothing red anywhere.

How the prototype behaves
  • Capture: tap the mic, it “hears” an example sentence, you stop and check the transcript, then analyze. Or type; the example chips route to each result.
  • «بكرا الساعة 9 دكتور وبعدين شغل» → two cards, each part of the sentence underlined in its card’s colour.
  • «لازم أسلّم التقرير» → one clarifying question as a bottom sheet (time missing).
  • «موعد مع سامي الخميس» → two readings: this Thursday or next.
  • «أدرس للامتحان» → review, then the first-move split.
  • «كيفك» → nothing found.
  • Saved has a 5‑second undo. Tapping any commitment opens details with تمّت / نعيد ترتيبها / أسقطه بوعي at equal weight.
  • Timeline “busy” blocks come from the phone calendar and never show titles.
